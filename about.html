Our story

Why BestaCare started

Most families don't plan for the day a parent comes home from hospital, or a newborn needs help round the clock. It usually arrives suddenly, often at night. Then comes the hard part: who can we trust in our home?

Across Mumbai, Pune and the rest of Maharashtra we saw families searching through agents and phone numbers, never sure who would turn up or whether they were trained. BestaCare began to change that.

We train every caretaker, nurse and attendant before they work with a family. We check their identity and background. And we stay in touch after they start, because good care needs someone who answers when you call.

Today we help families across Mumbai, Pune and the rest of Maharashtra with patient care, home nursing, elderly care and baby care, for a few hours, a night or round the clock, so you can go to work, rest or sleep knowing your loved one is in good hands.

Every family deserves care they don't have to worry about.

Sharif Khan, Founder
